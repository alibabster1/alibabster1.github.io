Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026. This policy covers takecontrolmindset.me and our prototype at takecontrolmindset.me/demo, run by Take Control Mindset (United States). Questions: ali@takecontrolmindset.me.

What we collect

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. You can use the site and the prototype without signing up, and we do not run tracking or advertising scripts.

What you type into the prototype. When you click Build the plan, your idea, notes and target length are sent to our backend, which passes them to Claude to write the plan and returns the result to your browser. Our backend does not save your text.

A daily request count. Our backend keeps one number per day, the total count of prototype requests, so we can cap overall use. It is not linked to you.

Your posting schedule stays on your device. Items you add to the posting schedule are stored in your own browser's local storage. We never receive them. Clear schedule, or clearing your browser data, deletes them.

Email. If you email us, we receive your message and address and use them only to reply.

Services that process requests

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Cloudflare Workers runs our prototype backend.
  • Hack Club AI currently relays prototype requests to Anthropic's Claude models, which generate the plan.

Like any web host, these providers may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver requests, under their own privacy policies.

Please do not enter personal information

The prototype is for video ideas. Do not type sensitive personal information into it. The site is intended for adults.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, for example when we add accounts or publishing, we will update this page and the date above first.
