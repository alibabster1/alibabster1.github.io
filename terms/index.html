The prototype

The prototype is an early, free product in development. It turns a video idea into a plan: talking points, a clip search plan, a timed edit plan, post copy and a posting schedule. Automatic clip search, rendering and publishing are not built yet. Features may change or stop working while we build.

Check the output before you post

The plans are written by AI and can be wrong. The prototype flags claims to fact-check, but you are responsible for checking facts and for what you publish. Make sure you have the rights to any clips, music or footage you use.
