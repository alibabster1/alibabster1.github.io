How it works

Five steps. One workflow.

Making one short video takes a creator hours: figuring out the angle, digging for clips, editing, writing the caption, remembering to post. We are putting all of it in one place. Each step below is marked with what works in our prototype today and what we are building next.

01

Idea

Live

The creator types an idea, a few notes and a target length of 15, 30 or 60 seconds.

02

Deep research

Live

Claude finds the angle that makes the video worth watching, lists the talking points, and flags any claim that should be fact-checked before posting.

03

Clip finding

Search plan liveAuto search in development

For every beat, what footage to look for and what it should show. Next: searching stock libraries and the creator's own clips automatically.

04

Detailed editing

Edit plan liveAuto render in development

A timed, beat-by-beat edit: shots, cuts, b-roll, on-screen text and sound cues. Next: rendering the vertical video straight from that plan.

05

Scheduled posting

Schedule liveAuto publish in development

Hook, caption and hashtags, placed in the next open posting slot. Next: publishing to Instagram automatically.

Prototype

Try it with your own idea.

Type a video idea and the prototype covers all five steps at the planning stage: research, a clip search plan, a timed edit plan, post copy and a spot on the posting schedule. It runs on Claude through our own backend.

Open the prototype ↗
  • What works today: research, clip search plan, timed edit plan, hook, caption, hashtags, posting schedule and CSV export.
  • What we are building next: automatic clip search, rendering the edit into a finished vertical video, and publishing to Instagram on schedule.
  • How we test it: on our own channel, @takecontrolmindset, before anything goes to other creators.
Built on Claude

Claude does the heavy lifting.

  • Today: Claude turns one idea into a full production plan: angle, talking points, fact-check flags, clip searches, edit beats and post copy.
  • Next: Claude coordinating the whole pipeline with tool use, calling clip search, editing and publishing tools in order and checking each step's output.
  • Why Claude: the plans have to be specific, careful with facts, and consistent with the creator's voice across many videos.
