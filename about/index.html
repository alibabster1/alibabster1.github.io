About

Take Control Mindset

Take Control Mindset is an early-stage startup based in the United States. We started operating on September 20, 2026. We are building one workflow that takes a short-form video from a creator's idea to a scheduled post: topic planning, clip finding, editing and posting, with Claude doing the heavy lifting at each step.

We started with our own channel, @takecontrolmindset on Instagram, because we kept running into the same problem: one short video takes hours of research, clip hunting, editing and caption writing before it is ever posted.

What works today, and what is next

Live: our prototype takes one video idea and a target length. Claude (Haiku 5.5, through our own backend) returns the angle, talking points, claims to fact-check, a search plan for every clip, a timed edit plan, and the hook, caption and hashtags. The post goes into a posting schedule that exports to CSV.

In development: automatic clip search, rendering the edit plan into a finished vertical video, and publishing to Instagram on schedule, with Claude coordinating each step through tool use.
